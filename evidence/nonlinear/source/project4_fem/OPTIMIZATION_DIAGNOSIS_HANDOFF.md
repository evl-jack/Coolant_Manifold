## Why SLSQP stalled

The frozen problem minimizes normalized viscous plus Brinkman dissipation at Re 50. Its 20 bounded fillet controls preserve the fixed ports and 1.00 mm minimum passage width. Side flow is measured as a share of inlet flow. The original side/main target of 0.25 corresponds to 20% of inlet flow, with an approved band of 19.5–20.5%. Pressure and diffuse solid area have separate upper limits. [Approved formulation](FORMULATION.md) · [Frozen demonstration protocol](optimization_demo/PROTOCOL.md)

| Metric | Original | SLSQP endpoint | Requirement |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Normalized dissipation | 1.000000 | 1.010156 | Minimize |
| Side flow as a share of inlet flow | 25.10111% | 24.21868% | 19.5–20.5% |
| Nondimensional pressure loss | 90.78080 | 92.33097 | ≤95.31984 |
| Editable diffuse solid area | 0.39662061 mm² | 0.39881273 mm² | ≤0.39662061 mm² |

The endpoint missed the upper flow limit by **3.71868 percentage points**, exceeded the area cap by **0.00219211 mm²** and raised dissipation by **1.01564%**. Pressure still had **2.98887** units of headroom. All 68 recorded PDE states passed the residual and mass gates. None satisfied all design constraints. SLSQP reported 22 iterations, 18 gradient bases and status 8, “Positive directional derivative for linesearch.” The endpoint is infeasible and there is no established constrained optimum. [Trace and results](optimization_demo/RESULTS.md) · [Optimizer return](optimization_demo/sol-2085871/math/termination.json)

The first SLSQP step changed a coefficient by 0.5, pushed 16 controls to bounds, and predicted essentially zero area change. The forward solve measured a **0.74828% area overrun**. Pure energy descent also tends to increase side flow. The energy and upper-flow gradient cosines were −0.893 at the original shape and −0.946 at the endpoint. The optimizer made most of its flow progress early. The final backtracking sequence shrank below a 10⁻¹¹ coefficient change with the side-flow violation still present. Recorded response predictions and an illustrative merit check agree at that tiny scale. The internal SLSQP merit model and its exact rejection decisions were unavailable. [Mathematical audit](optimization_diagnosis/MATH.md)

Seventeen controls ended within 10⁻⁵ of a bound. The lower-bound group was 5–8 and 14–19, and the upper-bound group was 2–4 and 10–13. Of the three remaining controls, control 9 had the meaningful flow sensitivity, and moving it toward less side flow worsened the area violation. A bounded linear program that includes inward moves from bounds gives a best predicted side share of **24.22145%** when it requires area and pressure compliance and caps all coefficient changes at 0.04. A diagnostic calculation omitting area reaches **24.21453%**, a reduction of only **0.00415 percentage points** from the endpoint. Row scaling can affect numerical conditioning and step selection. Positive rescaling leaves the permitted tangent steps unchanged. The trace supports a large early step followed by poor local geometry authority and an area/flow conflict. Feasibility elsewhere in the nonlinear design family remains open. [Geometry audit](optimization_diagnosis/GEOMETRY.md) · [Archived terminal linear program](optimization_demo/diagnostics/endpoint-tangent.json)

## What independent verification establishes

All **12** directional derivative and Taylor checks passed at the original 20-control shape. Independent reprocessing exactly reproduced the signed constraints at all 68 evaluations and the scaled Jacobians at all 18 gradient bases. The previously tested 0.04 energy-descent step reduced fitted-wall viscous dissipation by **0.05241%**, confirming local predictive capability. It worsened the already excessive side flow. The independently fitted SLSQP endpoint still gave **23.93718%** side flow, **3.43718 percentage points** above the upper limit. Its final mesh-refinement change was **0.00808 percentage points**, about 425 times smaller than the remaining miss. The fitted physical area and immersed diffuse-area constraint measure different quantities. These tests explain why the observed mesh sensitivity and the verified baseline derivatives are poor candidates for the failure. Near-bound gradients still lack independent perturbation verification, and the response Jacobian at the older seed has not been established by this diagnosis. ## Recommendation for approval

**Authorize one Sol job to evaluate the saved 2082990 design and test a bounded feasibility direction.** The older run performed a 35-iteration-capped side-flow search, so the useful new information is the local response at its *exact* saved vector under the frozen demonstration. There is no reason yet to repeat the old range search. [Archived seed and search history](output/sol-2082990/search-re-50.json)

1. Recompute the saved seed with the frozen L1 Re 50 model. Compare its measurements to the archive and require the original residual, mass, pressure, area, width and bounds checks. Record the four response gradients from the discrete adjoint for dissipation, side fraction, pressure and area. The two flow-constraint rows use the same side-fraction derivative with opposite signs.
2. Minimize affine upper-flow violation with an infinity-norm step limit of 0.04, true updated coefficient bounds and the original linearized lower-flow, pressure and area inequalities. Report the best predicted improvement against the **3.81331 percentage-point** shortfall. Use the larger of the seed replay's absolute side-fraction difference and the existing scaled primal tolerance converted to side-flow fraction (5×10⁻⁹) as a diagnostic resolution floor. If the predicted half-step improvement does not clear that floor, finish after the seed and its adjoint block. This floor leaves all original constraint and verification tests in force.
3. If there is a resolvable direction, cold-evaluate the half and full steps. Record predicted and measured changes in side flow, pressure, area and normalized dissipation, along with the state diagnostics. Apply the original lower-flow, pressure, area, width, bounds, mass and state gates. Forward reductions that are resolvable and admissible at both scales support a separate discussion of restoration. A discrepancy that changes the decision calls for a bound-admissible directional check before drawing a derivative conclusion. Any trial that remains above the flow ceiling is still outside the original design specification.

Use one job with **1 CPU, 4 GiB and a 2-minute wall cap**, at most three Re 50 design evaluations with a Stokes initialization for each cold state, and one adjoint block. Estimated execution is **5–15 seconds**, excluding queue time, based on archived timings. Reuse the immutable source and limit manifests, the recorded environment and one thread for BLAS and OpenMP. Archive the exact seed and input hashes, coefficients, the linear prediction, forward measurements, state gates and Slurm accounting. This serial allocation matches the sequential dependency of the pilot and avoids reserving extra cores for two small trials.

The pilot requires authorization and has not been submitted. A subsequent restoration campaign, optimizer benchmark, geometry change, changed PDE, altered engineering cap or revised verification threshold is outside this pilot. If the pilot shows no useful progress, return its evidence for a decision instead of proceeding automatically.
