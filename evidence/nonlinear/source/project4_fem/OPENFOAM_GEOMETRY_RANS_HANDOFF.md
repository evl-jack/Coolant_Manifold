# Coolant manifold next-stage decision handoff

## Decision

The metering reach improves the Re 50 flow split but has not produced a sampled design meeting all limits. The closest refined design that passes area, width, and pressure gives **21.2062%** side flow, **0.7062 percentage points above** the upper target, with only **0.0862 nondimensional pressure units** remaining. Review manufacturing clearance and the hardware head budget before extending geometry freedom. Keep the laminar and RANS results tied to their separate operating points and verification gates.

## Verified laminar FEM reference

The archived 26-control pilot **2083835** verified the map and the laminar adjoint in Stokes and Re 50. The independent wall-treatment refinement **2084088** retained its 24 verified solves. The fine fitted baseline and screened candidate gave **24.8871%** and **23.7106%** side flow, **91.1308** and **92.8386** static pressure loss, and **111.4011** and **112.5361** dissipation. Their paired side-flow reduction of **1.1765 points** exceeds the **0.1228-point** empirical grid-and-wall-treatment envelope for the paired gain. The finest Brinkman values were **25.3072%** and **24.2502%**. That comparison separates a repeatable paired geometry effect from the persistent absolute wall-treatment bias. The envelope is a numerical diagnostic. ## Independent laminar OpenFOAM

OpenCFD **v2512** `simpleFoam` solved the two archived 26-control shapes as one-cell-thick fitted meshes with empty front and back boundaries. Water properties and the Reynolds number set **U = 0.025095 m/s**. Both codes used the same prescribed parabolic inlet, fixed openings and measurement planes. OpenFOAM set outlet kinematic pressure to zero and the normal velocity gradient to zero. FEM imposed natural zero total traction for the symmetric Newtonian stress. Its signed-flow-weighted outlet pressure was **−0.4500** and **−0.4483** for the fine baseline and candidate, respectively. This is one measurable difference in the two outlet conditions. Both comparisons use inlet area mean minus combined signed-flow-weighted outlet pressure in the same units.

The first, native strip-prism series had poor face interpolation weights and nonorthogonality near the fillet. Its fine baseline settled near **22.50%**. The independently remeshed Delaunay-prism series preserves the same fitted-wall polygons and has better element quality. The Delaunay L1 (7,458 cells) baseline had **24.89°** maximum nonorthogonality versus **85.66°** on the native L1 series. Native results remain in the record as a mesh-quality finding.

### Analytical estimate versus measured feasibility

The friction-only long-reach screen predicted **+27.4%** branch resistance and a side share of **19.62%** using the fitted seed, or **20.07%** using the Brinkman seed. That calculation held the main-path resistance fixed and omitted compensation. The uncompensated FEM control retained essentially the predicted **0.50 mm²** area debit, but measured **20.5211%** side flow and exceeded the pressure cap. Widening the shoulder to restore area changes the junction loss and the flow split. The **21.2062%** upper-pocket result is the measured constrained screen. The finite DOE leaves broader feasible geometry and stationarity open. [Original analytical calculation](GEOMETRY_AND_RANS_DECISION_HANDOFF.md)

Its dimensional realization has a **20 mm gap**, **40 mm ideal wide-channel hydraulic diameter**, and **0.690795 m/s DNS bulk speed**. Its geometry, turbulence inflow and outlet behavior are separate. The current hardware depth, roughness and developed profiles were not provided. ## Coupled fitted-wall RANS and adjoint

The new fitted-wall FEM code adds P1 \(k,\omega\) transport to P2/P1 velocity and modified pressure. The assembled coupled tangent includes velocity-strain and eddy-viscosity derivatives in momentum, velocity convection and production derivatives in both transport equations, scalar cross-diffusion and blending, active limiter branches, and signed outlet scalar flux. The design derivative includes fitted mesh transforms, quadrature wall distance, wall spacing, and the omega Dirichlet lift. Element-local complex-step derivatives use the current active branches, with independent centered block/shape checks. The state Jacobian solved the transpose for three separate responses. Static pressure recovery includes the modeled isotropic stress term. The diagnostic mean-stress work counts transfer from the mean to turbulent kinetic energy once. [Coupled residual code](next_rans_adjoint/sst.py) · [Declared verification protocol](next_rans_adjoint/PROTOCOL.md)

Sol job **2084635** tested a **304-cell, 1,971-DOF manufactured fitted T** at numerical Re 2,000. Its fixed discrete load isolates the residual and derivative implementation. The scaled free residuals were ≤**7.22×10⁻¹⁴**, mass imbalance **5.55×10⁻¹⁶**, and the worst scalar balance error **1.12×10⁻¹³**. State and design Jacobian directional checks passed. The normalized transpose-product defect was **3.00×10⁻¹⁶**, and the independent assembly check was **2.56×10⁻¹⁶**. All **56** perturbed states reconverged.

At (k=0) and Re 50, job **2084895** recovered the unchanged laminar momentum and continuity residuals and their directional Jacobians to **5.52×10⁻¹⁶** relative. Across four jobs the RANS foundations used **0.07694 allocated CPU-hours**. ## Unverified components, approval gates, and presentation

The outstanding evidence is a pressure- and area-feasible geometry inside the target band, a physical head allowance and manufacturing tolerance, separated-flow and manifold turbulence validation, and full RANS gradient verification on a physical fitted-wall state. None can be replaced by a benchmark residual, a friction estimate or a coarse pressure pass.

1. Identify the hardware head allowance and reference planes, final coolant-area rule, permitted exterior stock, cutter radius, and minimum-width manufacturing tolerance. Reassess the neck and upper-pocket clearance together. The current 95.3198 cap and pilot area minimum remain the comparison limits.
2. Resolve the measured **0.7062-point** hydraulic gap with bounded options whose pressure and physical dimensions can be checked on refined fitted-wall meshes. Require a demonstrated feasible candidate before proceeding to stationarity or KKT reporting.
3. Confirm whether a separate RANS deliverable is required. Establish its real passage depth, inlet development and turbulence inputs, roughness, flow or pressure operating condition, and acceptable validation data. Require an unforced fitted-wall primal and all predeclared derivative gates before authorizing turbulence-consistent optimization. A later diffuse-wall RANS implementation additionally needs a verified differentiable turbulence wall model.
