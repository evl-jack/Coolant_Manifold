# Matched 26-control FEM verification protocol

Declared before submitting the matched study to Sol. This comparison repeats two saved shapes from job 2083835. The baseline and the lowest-side-flow Re 50 pilot shape that passed the area, pressure, and width screens supply both Stokes and Re 50 runs. Keep their 26 coefficients fixed at every mesh level. Evaluate Brinkman and explicit no-slip walls on the same three strip subdivisions, with refinement multipliers 1, 2, and 3 and 2,500, 10,000, and 22,500 triangles. Use the archived penalty 20,000 throughout. The fitted wall follows the analytical density midpoint contour, represented by straight mesh edges.

Use the same unit parabolic inlet, no-slip fixed walls, zero natural outlet traction, inlet height and mean inlet velocity scales, and identical evaluation planes at (x=-1), (x=21), and (y=11) mm. Integrate signed outlet flux and the same static pressure metric. Use Stokes to initialize Re 50 in each paired run. Report viscous dissipation for both representations and separately identify the Brinkman contribution. Report nominal-solid RMS speed as a fraction of mean inlet speed where `γ < 0.05` in immersed runs. The fitted fluid domain excludes this region. Report both exact physical area and mesh-integrated area changes, and compare the latter with the same method and mesh-level baseline.

## Acceptance criteria

- A state passes when its free-DOF nonlinear residual is at most `1e-11` and relative flux imbalance is below `1e-9`. Check fixed port planes and widths within `1e-12 mm`, unit inlet flux within `1e-12`, and minimum physical passage width at 1.00 mm. Retain failed solves and their reasons.
- Screen flow against 19.5%–20.5% and static pressure against the archived 95.31983921402909 cap using the existing scaled tolerance `1e-6`. Use no net reconstructed area loss and report representation-specific area screening using the same scaled tolerance as the pilot. For area integration agreement, require the absolute discrete-to-physical change on the finest grids to be at most 0.002 mm². This agreement threshold does not waive the mathematical area constraint.
- Diagnose grid resolution separately for each shape and wall treatment. Between levels 2 and 3 require a change of at most 0.10 percentage points in side fraction and 0.5% of the fine value in pressure loss and total dissipation. For agreement between the two wall treatments, report against the same thresholds. Require the level 2 to 3 change in leakage to be at most 10% of the fine Brinkman RMS value. Leakage is a diagnostic with no physical threshold specified.
- Define a positive design gain as baseline side fraction minus candidate side fraction, in percentage points, within one method and level. Let (U_g) be the larger absolute change in gain from levels 2 to 3 across the two methods. Let (U_w) be the absolute difference between the two finest-grid gains. Use (U_g+U_w) as a conservative empirical resolution check. The gain is resolved if both methods show a positive fine-grid gain exceeding this sum, all four fine states pass, and the side-fraction grid checks pass for each shape. Record absolute wall-treatment bias separately. The envelope is a diagnostic based on these grids without a formal asymptotic error estimate.
