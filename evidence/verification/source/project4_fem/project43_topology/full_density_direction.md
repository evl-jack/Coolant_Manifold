# Full-density topology optimization

The 20-variable experiments are suspended. Their source, diagnostics and Sol results remain part of the project record. The archived full-density MMA pilot passed first-order checks for the immersed problem. At the fixed 0.5 threshold, its main outlet belongs to a different component from the inlet and side outlet. A physical design must pass connectivity and an independent fitted-wall flow-split check.

## Proposed differentiable connectivity certificate

Use the same cell-center samples of projected fluid density \(\bar\gamma\) that enter the existing reconstruction. Join centers of face-adjacent cells. Attach inlet, main-outlet and side-outlet terminals only through design-boundary samples whose constant-normal extension overlaps the respective exact fixed-fluid collar over positive width. No corner-only edges are present.

Fix a density margin \(\delta>0\) and a transition width \(w>0\). Let \(b_i=S((\bar\gamma_i-0.5-\delta)/w)\), with \(S(t)=0\) for \(t\le0\), \(S(t)=1\) for \(t\ge1\), and \(S(t)=6t^5-15t^4+10t^3\) in between. Terminal values have \(b=1\). Define the auxiliary edge conductance \(k_{ij}=\epsilon_{ij}+\kappa_{ij} b_i b_j\) with strictly positive \(\epsilon_{ij},\kappa_{ij}\). The graph uses density to determine conductance and is independent of the physical velocity and Brinkman resistance.

For each outlet \(t\) separately, minimize \(E_t=\sum_{(i,j)} k_{ij}(\phi_i-\phi_j)^2\) over all potentials with inlet potential 1 and outlet \(t\) potential 0. Leave the other terminal floating. Every edge is counted once. The free-node equations are the weighted graph Laplacian \(\sum_j k_{ij}(\phi_i-\phi_j)=0\). These Dirichlet systems are positive definite and satisfy the discrete maximum principle, \(0\le\phi\le1\). The minimized energy \(C_t\) equals the net inlet current.

Require \(C_t\ge C_{\min,t}>\sum_e\epsilon_e\) for each outlet. If no positive-gated path reaches the outlet, set a trial potential to 1 on the inlet's reachable set and 0 outside it. It satisfies the Dirichlet conditions and charges only floor edges across the cut. The minimized energy is therefore at most \(\sum_{e\in\mathrm{cut}}\epsilon_e\), which contradicts the constraint. Both passing constraints certify face-connected paths whose cell-center values strictly exceed 0.5. Each connecting segment lies on a marching-squares patch edge with both endpoints strictly above threshold. The positive margin and seam overlap give positive-width connections in a valid reconstructed polygon. The floating terminal represents passage within its single connected fixed-fluid collar. Independently verify both outlets on the resulting polygons. The certificate supplies no minimum manufacturable channel width and does not establish the physical flow split.

At the potential minimizer, derivatives through free potentials cancel because their energy gradient vanishes. Thus

\[
 \frac{\partial C_t}{\partial \bar\gamma_i}
 = b'_i \sum_{j:(i,j)} \kappa_{ij} b_j(\phi_i-\phi_j)^2.
\]

Pull the resulting covector back through the existing projection derivative and Helmholtz transpose. A scaled inequality for MMA is \(1-C_t/C_{\min,t}\le0\). A uniform floor is useful for pilot conditioning, with threshold and floor specified together so their separation remains valid on each graph. A sufficient graph certificate can reject geometries connected by paths that off-center samples capture. Also, a disconnected region can have a zero derivative where gates saturate, making restoration from disconnected seeds unreliable. Test strict-positive margin and sensitivity from the original binary T at each continuation stage. Retain the exact reconstruction check at all accepted candidates. Production adoption requires explicit review of this formulation and of the test evidence.

The pilot uses \(\delta=0.025\), \(w=0.15\), total floor budget \(10^{-4}\), and \(C_{\min,t}=10^{-3}\) for both outlets. Sol array 2399203 passed the exact reconstruction, graph-cut, and gradient tests. Main-outlet conductance was 0.40074 for the sharp T and \(2.10\times10^{-7}\) for the archived disconnected MMA endpoint. The adjoint identity error was \(5.55\times10^{-17}\) and the finest raw-density finite difference error was \(1.56\times10^{-10}\). The gray barrier passed a naive positive-conductance test and failed the certified test.

The two initial graph-constrained pilots in array 2399238 produced no feasible handoff. In array 2399311, graph inequalities remain hard during Phase I while the existing hydraulic and area inequalities can use the restoration slack. A zero-slack feasible design still satisfies every original inequality and both graph constraints. The first pilots and all optimizer histories remain archived.

## First Sol results

- At the original 0.5 mm design spacing, the original and expanded envelopes have 240 and 280 design cells. Both sharp initializations retain 83 mm² of raw fluid and unchanged ports. The expanded filter moves 2.18758 mm² of projected fluid into the added strip at beta zero and conserves the total projected area. This redistribution precedes any optimization and must be accounted for in the comparison.
- The original projection-only route tested beta 0, 1, 2, 4 and 8 at \(\alpha_{\max}=10^5\). Its selected endpoint at each stage disconnected the main outlet at the fixed reconstruction threshold. The resistance-only route reached a connected candidate at \(10^6\) with reconstructed area 80.43056 mm² and 49.90011% immersed side flow. Its fitted side flow on the three levels was 62.27054%, 62.64846% and 62.75367%. It failed both successive split checks and the physical flow band.
- The selected expanded-envelope projection candidate at beta 8 was connected and had reconstructed area 76.23990 mm². Its immersed side flow was 49.90001%. The three fitted levels gave 81.43768%, 81.80011%, and 81.98012%. It failed the physical flow band and the two successive split checks. The effect of expanding the allowed design region is distinct from a validated change in performance.
- In the planar channel at \(10^7\), the one-layer correction reduced nominal flux error from 1.50901% to 0.00475% on the finest mesh. In the sharp manifold fixture at \(10^6\), it reduced the fitted split discrepancy from 0.58291 to 0.13159 percentage points and power discrepancy from 1.47146% to 0.11593%. In the obstructed fixture, it reduced those errors from 0.43177 to 0.14451 percentage points and from 3.79945% to 0.37255%. Corrected nominal gross ingress remained 0.34803% in the sharp fixture and 1.69282% in the obstructed fixture. Both fixtures still failed the 0.02-percentage-point split and 0.10% ingress gates.

## Stopping point

- Sol 2399203 completed. In the original coupled sweep, the connected beta 2 and 4 candidates at \(10^6\) gave 61.09974% and 59.49378% on the finest fitted checks. The beta 8 geometry was connected and exceeded the reconstructed area cap by 0.01029 mm². None passed the physical gate.
- Sol 2399311 completed. Neither hard-connectivity restoration gave a feasible handoff at \(10^5\). The best logged trials that passed the two graph constraints and area gave 27.90666% side flow in the original envelope and 19.31365% in the expanded envelope. These are bounded pilot results.
- Sol 2399238 was still running at the stopping point. The corrected sharp fixture at \(10^7\) passed the per-fixture checks on one locally resolved analysis mesh. Split discrepancy fell from 0.168779 to 0.018995 percentage points, power discrepancy from 0.462044% to 0.019172%, gross ingress to 0.048910% and the maximum through-solid cut flux to 0.017595%. The matching obstructed case and any further local convergence assessment remain outstanding.
- Sol 2399522 was still running. This final already-submitted two-task diagnostic minimizes squared distance to the 50% split at \(10^6\), with the area and two graph constraints hard. Both tasks start independently from the sharp T and check the original flow band and reconstructed geometry after the search. There is no Phase II power optimization in this diagnostic.
- The verified FEM, Newton, adjoint, MMA/CCSA and KKT implementations remain the basis of the study. Production and the Stokes comparison remain gated on physical validation of the nonlinear workflow. No reduced-density jobs were launched for this direction.
